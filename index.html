Who we are

More than a fraternity.
A launchpad.

Alpha Kappa Psi is the oldest and largest professional business fraternity in the world. The Beta Omicron chapter brings that legacy to Wayne State University, where students from every major come together to sharpen their skills, build lasting friendships, and open doors they didn't know existed.

We're a co-ed organization open to students of all majors who share a drive to grow professionally and personally. Our brothers go on to careers in finance, consulting, marketing, tech, entrepreneurship, healthcare, and more, and they stay connected long after graduation.

Questions

Things people
usually ask.

Do I have to be a business major?

No. Alpha Kappa Psi is open to students of every major. Many of our brothers study engineering, health sciences, communications, and the liberal arts. What we look for is drive and character, not a specific degree.

Is it co-ed?

Yes. Alpha Kappa Psi has been co-ed since 1976. Everyone is a "brother" regardless of gender, and that's a tradition we're proud of.

How much time does it take?

Expect a weekly chapter meeting plus a handful of events per month. Brothers earn points across our four pillars each semester, and you can plan around your class and work schedule. The pledge semester is more involved, and we're upfront about that during rush.

Are there dues?

Yes. Dues cover national membership, chapter events, and operating costs. The exact amount is shared during rush, and we work with brothers who need a payment plan.

Professional vs. social fraternity?

Our focus is career and character development. We still have plenty of social events, but every semester is built around professional growth, service, and leadership, not just social life.

Can I rush as a freshman or a senior?

Freshmen are welcome to rush. Seniors can too, though we'll talk through whether the pledge timeline works with your graduation date.
